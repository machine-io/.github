# machine-io

**AI for manufacturing** — bringing modern AI to the shop floor.

制造业 + AI：把现代 AI 带到生产一线。

## What we do

Manufacturing runs on hard-won, hands-on knowledge — and most of it never
makes it into software. We collect real pain points from the industry and
turn them into open specs and tools.

制造业依赖大量一线沉淀的经验知识，而其中大部分从未进入软件系统。我们从行业
一线收集真实痛点，把它们转化为开放的规格文档和工具。
